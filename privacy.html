Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. About the Application

Shymkent Monitoring is a media monitoring and analytics application focused on public discussions about Shymkent, Kazakhstan.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the permissions granted and the features used, the application may process publicly available Threads posts, text, usernames, publication dates, post links and related metadata obtained through authorized APIs.

3. Purpose of Processing

Information is used to identify public concerns, analyze discussion topics, categorize messages, and produce aggregated analytics about city issues.

4. Data Storage and Security

We aim to limit access to collected information, use it only for the stated purposes, and protect stored information against unauthorized access.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by technical service providers necessary to operate the application, subject to applicable requirements.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained only as necessary for the application's purposes and applicable obligations. Requests concerning personal information or its deletion can be submitted using the contact below.

7. Third-Party Platforms

The application may use Meta Threads APIs and other authorized sources. Platform data is subject to applicable platform terms and policies.
